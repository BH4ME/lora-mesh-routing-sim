# ICC 2027 Comparison

This report summarizes the verified four-scenario ICC matrix generated by
`tools/run_icc_experiments.sh` and stored under
`results/meshecho_v2_1_0_icc_50n_*`.

For these runs, `unicast_pdr` means source-side ACK-confirmed PDR, while
`destination_unicast_pdr` tracks DATA arrival at the destination.

All scenarios use 50 nodes, a 3000 m square, 1200 s duration, 8 fixed unicast
pairs, SF9/BW125 kHz/CR 4/5, and 20 seeds.

## Takeaway

- Smart-CALM consistently improves ACK-confirmed reliability over CALM and
  MeshCore-like.
- The gain comes with a much larger tail ACK delay and a noticeably larger
  fallback budget than CALM.
- `smart-calm-no-fallback` shows the reliability drop when rescue flooding is
  removed.
- `smart-calm-no-confidence` matches `smart-calm` in the first three scenarios,
  so the current random topologies do not strongly stress the confidence-ranking
  branch.
- The high-load scenario exposes the sharpest reliability-vs-delay tradeoff.

The full raw CSVs and long-format summaries also include energy, control
overhead, and per-seed confidence intervals.
