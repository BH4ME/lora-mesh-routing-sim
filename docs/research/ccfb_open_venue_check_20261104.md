## Availability and source conflicts

- SECON official EDAS link N35491 redirects anonymous visitors to login.
  A future CFP deadline is confirmed, but the authenticated submission form
  was not inspected. Instructions: 9 pages inclusive, IEEE 10pt, double blind.
- CoNEXT winter HotCRP exists but says "Submissions are currently closed."
  This is a published future-deadline preparation target, not a claim that
  uploads are enabled today. Papers publish in PACMNET and present at CoNEXT.
  Long papers: 16 single-column pages; short papers: 10; references excluded.
- MobiSys linked HotCRP says "No such conference". Future deadlines are
  explicit in its CFP. Instructions specify single-blind review, 12-page
  ACM 10pt body. The CFP emphasizes working/reproducible implementations
  and rigorous practical evaluation.
- SenSys static CFP still says October 29 abstract and November 5 full
  paper, AoE. The linked live system /deadlines explicitly permits new
  registrations until November 4 07:59:59 EST and full submission until
  November 6 06:59:59 EST. These convert to the Beijing times above.
  This discrepancy is reported, not explained by an assumed extension.
  The system was still open when checked. SenSys is not recommended as a
  rushed target for the present simulation-only manuscript.

## Recommendation

SECON is the primary practical fit: mesh/IoT/low-power networking is in
scope, theoretical and applied research are invited, and the meeting is in
the first half of 2027. No universal hardware prerequisite appears in the CFP.
The present 20-node, 20-seed, single-case simulation still needs stronger
baselines, varied workloads/topologies and component ablations.
CoNEXT is another strong protocol fit requiring a stronger research case.
MobiSys and SenSys fit the wireless/LPWAN domain but the present lack of
practical systems evaluation is a larger limitation.

ICNP 2027 and MobiHoc 2027 official CFPs were not verified; their series
pages currently list 2026 as the latest edition. IWQoS 2027 returns HTTP 401
password protection. Do not invent deadlines or label these confirmed open.
